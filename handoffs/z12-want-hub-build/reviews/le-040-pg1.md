## 2. Is the evidence sufficient for "the shared hot path Syl's process runs is unchanged on the default path" on REAL graphs?
Verdict: **COMPLIANT for what it claims; the claim must be read narrowly.** PG-1 PASS is an equivalence result for a direct call of the default path on two specific real graphs. It is strong where it reaches and silent elsewhere. What it proves and what it does not:

[is] identical". The records do not show that any counter moved.
