## 9. FLAGS (not decided by me; for the Chief / Josh; ranked)
- **F1 (HIGH-ish, ~70% by the law review; mine too): a drain pass concurrent with an in-flight merge cannot see that merge's arrivals.** The ruling's held set is cohort ∪ `merge_landed` of THE asking merge, and the daemon's drain passes no `merge_landed` (the brief said so explicitly). `merge_cc_topology` releases `_step_lock` between batches, the daemon does not serialize merges against the drain (its own `_drain_precheck_blocked` docstring says so), and an arrival whose binding is in a LATER batch is unbound meanwhile. Before this change the drain's whole-graph hold covered it; now a drain pass running at that moment can age it through its steps. Proposed fix, AT THE SOURCE and outside this brief: NG keeps a process-wide registry of in-flight merges' `merge_landed` sets (added on entry, removed in a `finally`), and `held_unbound_nodes` unions them when `merge_landed is None`; the daemon would not change. NOT built; recorded in the `held_unbound_nodes` docstring and the daemon changelog.
