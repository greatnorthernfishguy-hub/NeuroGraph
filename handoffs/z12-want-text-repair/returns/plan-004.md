### 4.2 The algorithm, per node `N` in `S` (`T = want_text`, `C = vdb.content[source_node]`)
1. **Source (A0).** `source_node` is a graph node that is not a want, not constitutional, not `*_authored`, and `C` exists; else `source_missing` → unchanged, listed.
2. **Anchor (A1).** `C.count(T) == 1` at `p`; `C[:p].rstrip()` ends with `WANT_OPEN`, the outer opener at `i`; after `T`, skipping whitespace, `WANT_CLOSE` follows at `j`; **`closer_end = j + len(WANT_CLOSE)`** (7; the constant is imported, not typed). Else `anchor_failed` → unchanged, listed.
3. **Parse.** `wp = parse_wants(C)`, once per distinct source (49 distinct [derived]; cached).
4. **Decide by the want that closes at `j`** (`w` = the entry of `wp.wants` with `w.close_end == closer_end`):
   - **GENUINE** — `w.open_start == i` and `w.text == T`: the old span **is** a legitimate want (it may legitimately be long, and under F3 may contain masked mention pairs). **UNCHANGED**; assert `want_id_for_text(T) == N.id` (else `id_mismatch`, listed).
   - **SEPARATE** — `w.open_start > i`: the legitimate want is a nested pair; `X = w.text`. **Candidate**, subject to the collision rule, the approval gate (§5) and fidelity (V13). No length test of any kind.
   - **ANOMALY** — `w` exists but `w.open_start < i`, or `w.open_start == i` and `w.text != T`: `pair_spans_beyond_old_span` / `function_disagrees_with_anchor` → unchanged, listed.
   - **NONE** — no want closes at `j` **and no minted want overlaps the old span** `[i, closer_end)`: every marker in the span was skipped; reasons recorded. The span is discussion; nothing to separate. **UNCHANGED, LISTED** with those reasons.
   - **[R4·own] OVERLAP-ANOMALY** — no want closes at `j`, but some minted want overlaps the old span and closes elsewhere (le-021 **N1**: an unpaired mention inside a real want followed by any later closer mints a truncated, marker-bearing fragment that ends beyond `j`; also a want reaching in from before `i`). → `overlap_other_pair`: unchanged, listed, and the overlapping want goes to the marker-bearing review (§4.4-b). Plan-003 would have called these NONE.
5. **No guessing.** `X` is always `w.text`; no proxy decides a node.
6. **[R4·C17-4/LE15-C12] Post-conditions and `assert_failed`.** For every SEPARATE candidate: `X == C[w.open_start+len(WANT_OPEN) : w.close_end-len(WANT_CLOSE)].strip()`, `X in T`, `T.endswith(X)`, `X` non-empty, `want_id_for_text(X)` equals the mapping's `new_id`.
