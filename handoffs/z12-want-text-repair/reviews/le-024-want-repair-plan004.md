## B2 - Is the per-id Executive-signed review a real gate?
**Yes, as a mechanical fail-closed gate; no, as authentication of a person - and the plan says so (§5.2 "Limit, stated").** What makes it real: the write condition binds the id, `decision == approved`, a run-time recomputed `excerpt_sha256` (with the scrub version in it), `x_sha16`/`t_sha16`, the function pin, `repair_list_sha256`, `scope_ids_sha256`, and the approvals file's own sha256 relayed by Chief in a dispatch; V17 asserts BOTH directions (`written == approved ∩ passed` and nothing outside the approvals). **Findings**
- **B2-F1 (MEDIUM) - the excerpt the Executive signs against is under-specified for the decision being made.** A SEPARATE decision is about the INNER (legitimate) opener `w.open_start` and what is dropped between the outer opener and it. §4.2-7/§5.1 give "the +/-80-character excerpts at the opener and closer and the printed `X`" - "the opener" is ambiguous (outer `i` or inner). **Fix:** state the excerpt anchors as outer opener, inner opener (`open_start`), closer, plus printed `X` in full, the removed-prefix length, and that `excerpt_sha256` covers all of them.
- **B2-F2 (MEDIUM) - Phase 1 to Phase 2 drift is handled two ways.** §4.2-6 says a node that fails a post-condition "is removed from the write set and the mapping is recomputed"; §5.2/P5 say the run's `repair_list_sha256`/`scope_ids_sha256`/mapping must equal the approved values. If a node passes in Phase 1 and fails on the live bytes, "remove and continue" would silently shrink the approved repair while the hash pin says STOP. **Fix:** at Phase 2 any deviation from the approved list is a STOP for the whole run (recompute-and-drop is Phase-1 behaviour only).
