**AMENDED 2026-07-28 — the flat "never bidirectional" constraint is WITHDRAWN and replaced by a two-clause additive-only test.** Josh challenged the direction-count framing and was right: direction count was a *proxy*, and an inconsistent one — I had already blessed many modules (Immunis, TID wire, CC) depositing multi-directionally into a shared Commons without demanding "one direction only." The violation was never the direction; it is **agreement-seeking**: computing a diff and applying deletions/authoritative overwrites so two stores come to match, which requires one side to hold authority over the other's state. **Governing test, clause 1:** does either direction delete, overwrite, or diff against the other side's state? Yes → dead form. No → additive donation, clean regardless of direction count. Peninsula precedent applies (additive-deposit-no-authority was always the approved property; the DEAD transmit form's defect was the `to=` addressed-recipient authority, not its direction).

**BUT clause 1 alone is necessary-not-sufficient — I add clause 2: the ECHO test.** Two additive, deletion-free, authority-free legs still produce a real harm clause 1 cannot see. If the laptop's reverse leg cannot distinguish *laptop-originated* topology from *VPS-donated topology it absorbed*, absorbed trees get re-exported upstream and the VPS re-absorbs its own output as novel experience. Every deposit in that loop is additive and authority-free, yet in a Hebbian substrate repeated re-deposit of the same topology manufactures **fake salience** — the substrate reads its own reflection as genuine repetition. That is LAW-7 harm (experience that is not experience). **The mechanism is already latent in this design, verified 2026-07-28:** `PyFiredNode` carries NO metadata, so provenance is dropped in transit; and node ids are host-neutral (`"cc:conv::" + sha1(text)`, cc_ng_organism.py:1366/3012 — no host tag; `instance_tag` reaches only trisynaptic handoff filenames/systemd scopes, never ids or metadata). So an absorbed node is today indistinguishable from a locally-grown one. **Clause 2:** absorb MUST re-stamp origin provenance, and every export MUST filter to locally-originated only — a positive origin whitelist, never a blacklist (same shape as [[ruling-coresident-cc-export-scoping]]).

**How to apply:** a future laptop→VPS leg is evaluated against BOTH clauses, not against a direction count. Clause 2 is the one that will actually bite, and it must be satisfied *before* any reverse leg, because the provenance re-stamp has to be in place at absorb time on the leg being built NOW. Weaker watch-item (noted, not a violation): indefinite two-way accretion converges the hemispheres by union rather than by agreement-seeking; receiver plasticity + probation make this lossy so they don't literally converge, but if the hemispheres ever stop differing, revisit.
